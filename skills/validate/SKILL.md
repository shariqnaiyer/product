---
name: validate
description: Deep-research a product idea before building it. Use when the user shares an idea and wants it validated or pressure-tested.
---

Spin up **one background agent per question**, in parallel. Each researches the idea against real evidence (forums, reviews, pricing pages, job posts) and cites every claim.

1. **How often does it hurt?** Weekly beats yearly.
2. **What does it cost them now** in money, hours or risk?
3. **What do they do about it today?** If nothing, it isn't painful.
4. **Who pays, and do they have a budget?**
5. **How will you reach the first 100?**

Then write a verdict (**build**, **narrow** or **kill**) with one line per question and the cheapest no-code test of the weakest answer. Save it to `knowledge/ideas/` in the product repo.
